# New Hire Ramp SOP

Oct 30, 2026 · @Sheila

## Purpose and scope

This SOP is how SMB Deal Hunter takes a new closer from hire to the end of ramp month 3, and decides at each gate whether they move on, go on watch, or are let go. It covers two tracks: **new hire closers** and **setter promotes** (setters moving up to closer). Every stage moves on readiness, not on a fixed calendar.

| Who | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Sheila | The roster (names and dates), the roadmap rules, keep-or-cut decisions with Jay |
| Jay | Mock calls, call reviews, feedback, approval onto the closer calendar, keep-or-cut decisions with Sheila |
| Claude | Pulling and checking the data, the daily alert, the Monday digest, office hour prep, chasing Jay's feedback at checkpoints |

Everything posts to #new-hire-onboarding, a Slack channel with only Sheila and Jay. New hires are not in it.

## Key dates and definitions

Three dates drive everything, and they are entered on the Roster tab of the New Hire Tracker. Ramp months are counted from go-live, not by calendar month.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Org start | First day of onboarding. Week 1 starts on this exact day, so a Friday starter's week 1 runs Friday to Thursday. |
| Closer calendar go-live | The date of the first 45-minute discovery call actually held. Jay approves the move; this is the date it took effect. |
| Ramp month N | Runs from go-live plus N−1 months to the day before go-live plus N months. Go-live 10/15 means month 1 is 10/15–11/14. |
| Ascension start | Setter promotes only: first day of promote training or the first cohort call. |
| First self-set call | Setter promotes only: the first closer call they booked to themselves. Pre-ramp month 1 starts here. |
| Paused clock | A setter promote's pre-ramp clock stops while they are away and restarts when they return. |
| Booked call | A 45-minute discovery call on the rep's calendar in Close, whether or not the prospect showed. |
| Showed call | A discovery call where the prospect showed up. Counted from EOD reports, capped at the day's booked discovery calls. |
| Setter call | A 15–20 minute intro call. If a setter call turns into a pitch and runs about 45 minutes, it counts as a closer call. |
| Call-to-close (CTC) | Closes ÷ showed calls. |

## New hire closer track

About two weeks of pre-ramp, then three ramp months on the closer calendar, each with a cash floor that decides keep or cut.

**Pre-ramp (about 2 weeks, readiness-gated)**

1. Day 1: added to a Slack channel with Sheila and Jay; sent the self-learning links and the new hire tracker.
2. Week 1: self-guided learning (Guru, Kajabi, recent call recordings). Submits a call audit: grades one top closer's recording with the call review rubric.
3. End of week 1 or early week 2: 45-minute mock discovery call, Jay as the client. The new hire self-reviews the recording against the rubric; Jay gives feedback on the key areas to improve, which are logged for later checks.
4. Week 2, setter week: 15-minute setter calls following the setter SOP. Floor: 6 booked a day, 4 live a day (80% show), 20 live calls for the week. Picks 1–2 setter calls for Jay to review at cohort office hours. Must keep the CRM up to date and file setter EOD reports.
5. Graduation: once the call audit, self-review, Jay's feedback, setter calls, CRM and EODs are all clear, Jay approves them for the closer calendar. The first discovery call held is go-live.

**Ramp months on the closer calendar**

| Month | Scheduled calls | Cash target | Cash floor | CTC target | If below the floor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 60 open slots | $100K | $75K | 12–15% | Out (discretionary, see below) |
| 2, open path (M1 ≥ $100K) | Up to 75 | $200K | $150K | 20% | Out |
| 2, throttled path (M1 < $100K) | 60 | $150K | $150K | 20% | Out |
| 3 | 75 | $200K | $200K and 25% CTC | 25% | Out if either is missed |

- **Near miss:** month 1 cash of $95K–$99.9K is flagged to Sheila to decide the month 2 path.
- **Checkpoints:** if issues Jay flagged are still there at the 5th showed call, the rep goes on watch; still there at the 10th, they are out.
- **Keep-or-cut is discretionary.** Cash is the headline, but the call is made against how many calls the rep actually got and where in the calendar month they went live, compared with past hires at the same point (Kevin and Daniel were kept for this reason).

## Setter promote track

Setter promotes earn the closer calendar through two self-set pre-ramp months; after go-live they follow the same ramp months, targets and cuts as new hire closers.

1. Training: the same materials, call audit and mock call with Jay as new hire closers. There is no separate setter week.
2. Self-set phase: they keep taking inbound setter calls and book closer calls to themselves. Daily mix: about 5–6 booked setter calls and 1–2 closer calls.
3. Pre-ramp months (clock starts at the first self-set call and pauses for time off):

| Pre-ramp month | Closer calls | CTC target | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | About 20 | 15% | Enough calls to judge |
| 2 | About 40 | 15% | Below 15% CTC: not ascended |

4. Ascension: if pre-ramp month 2 clears, Jay puts them fully on the closer calendar. Go-live and ramp months 1–3 then work exactly as in the new hire closer track.

**Watch-out:** low self-set volume is the main failure mode (Quinn, Yobani and Dave today). How long pre-ramp takes varies by person, so a promote who stays in pre-ramp without enough closer calls to judge is flagged.

**Comparison group:** current promotes are compared with past promotes Jay Z (Jordan Zadrozny), EJ (Ezra Isla) and Omar (Omar Kaddoura) at the same pre-ramp and ramp months.

## Call review and feedback loop

Jay's feedback is the basis for the 5th and 10th call checkpoints, so every piece of it gets written down in #new-hire-onboarding and tracked until it is fixed.

1. **Rubric:** all reviews use the latest call review rubric (the sheet ending ...GsicTc78), not the old "Call Review" tab.
2. **Call audit and self-review:** the new hire grades a top closer's call before the mock, then grades their own mock recording. Jay checks the self-review shows they understand what matters.
3. **Jay's mock feedback:** logged as open items per rep (for example, Oliver: rebate explained wrong; Tyler: more personality in discovery).
4. **Checkpoint reviews:** when a rep reaches their 5th and 10th showed closer call, Claude tags Jay in the channel and asks him to review one recent call against the open items.
5. **Follow-up:** if Jay hasn't replied, Claude re-pings him in the thread each morning until he does, then logs each item as fixed, still open, or new.
6. **Setter call reviews:** in setter week, the rep picks 1–2 calls for Jay to review at cohort office hours, live or async.

Jay can post feedback in a reply thread in any format, for example: *Oliver, call with X 10/28. Fix: rushed price. Better: discovery.*

## Monitoring cadence

Three posts go to #new-hire-onboarding, and each one only says what Sheila or Jay need to act on.

| Post | When | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily alert | 7:00 AM ET, weekdays | One line per rep who needs action: a checkpoint call review due (Jay tagged), a ramp month ending within 7 days, a setter-week day short of 6 booked or 4 live, a setter promote with no self-set closer calls for 5 weekdays, a floor at risk, a near miss to decide, a date that disagrees with its source. On a quiet day, one line saying nothing needs you. |
| Monday digest | 7:30 AM ET, Mondays, separate post | Each ramping rep compared with past reps at the same day of the same ramp month (booked, showed, closes, cash, CTC), as an image; setter promote self-set volume by pre-ramp month. |
| Office hour prep | 24 hours before each cohort session | Weekly per cohort until the cadence is set. Format to be reworked with Sheila. |

Empty sections are left out; nothing is posted just to fill space.

## Data sources and rules

Each number has one main source and a backup, and conflicts are flagged rather than silently picked.

| Metric | Main source | Backup / cross-check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roster and dates | Roster tab, New Hire Tracker | Close, Slack, Google Calendar, Calendly |
| Booked discovery calls | Close (45-minute meetings) | Calendly, Google Calendar |
| Setter calls and self-sets | Close | Calendly |
| Showed calls, offers, closes | EOD tracker | Sales Tracker |
| Cash collected | 2M tracker (read-only) | Sales Tracker daily inputs |
| Calendar capacity | Messi reports in #sales-reports | Calendly |
| Jay's feedback | #new-hire-onboarding, Granola notes | Review sheets |

**Known gaps**

- Close can't show whether a prospect actually showed, or how long a call ran. Zoom recording length would settle both, and Zoom is not connected yet.
- EOD reports are missing some days; on those days shows are left uncounted, not guessed.
- Some reps log setter calls as closer calls in their EODs; shows are capped at the day's booked discovery calls until Zoom lengths are available.
- The Weekly/Monthly Report numbers mix conventions (go-live window, first-call window, calendar month); one convention still needs to be chosen.
- The 2M tracker is never modified; it is read only.
